Summary
- Quality was equal on this suite; moving vision embedding to the Mac did not reduce vLLM quality.
- The split request was 7.06x larger because BF16 visual vectors were larger than the compressed source images.
- Full-H200 pipeline TTFT was 100.93 ms faster (27.6%).
- Full-H200 pipeline E2E was 97.09 ms faster (10.8%).
- Removing the H200 vision embedder saved about 0.10 GiB of loaded model memory.

This is a small controlled regression suite, not a general model-quality benchmark.
