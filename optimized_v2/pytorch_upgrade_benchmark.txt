Conclusion
- Matching the exact PyTorch commit, Torchvision version, and Transformers
  version did not make MPS and CUDA bit-identical.
- Split accuracy increased by three samples relative to the prior split run and
  was two samples higher than full H200 on this 100-sample subset.
- The version-matched split run had higher TTFT. This single ChartQA run does not
  isolate whether that came from PyTorch, machine load, or runtime conditions.
- Full H200 was not rerun because its CUDA environment did not change; its first
  100 records were derived from the existing 200-sample run.
- The prior stage-level check used a different image, so its differing-value
  counts cannot be used as a controlled before/after comparison.
- The exploratory PyTorch 2.13 result is retained in
  benchmark-results/split-chartqa-100-torch213, but it is not the primary
  full-versus-split comparison because the H200 remained on PyTorch 2.11.
