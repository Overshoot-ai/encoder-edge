Conclusion
- Aggregate relaxed accuracy was identical on this 200-sample subset.
- Full and split execution are not bit-identical.
- Small MPS/CUDA numerical differences can change greedy generation trajectories.
- The equal aggregate score hides 24 samples whose correctness outcome flipped direction.
- This is a 200-sample subset, not the full 2500-sample official ChartQA run.
